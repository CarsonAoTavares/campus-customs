# Storefront Design

The storefront pairs Yale cobalt with coral and sunny gold, using a campus editorial hierarchy and compact collectible-drop labels. Real catalog photography anchors the hero and product cards; small sticker-like accents and restrained reveal/hover motion add personality without obscuring the merchandise.

Search and garment-type filters make a large catalog easier to scan. Clear prices, brief descriptions, visible stock by size, and clickable product cards reduce uncertainty and take shoppers directly to complete product details. The persistent, page-aware chat offers quick prompts and linked recommendations, helping customers find an item and move naturally toward a purchase.
